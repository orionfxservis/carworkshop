Cookie Policy

An overview of how Orion Fx uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Last Updated — August 16, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly and can be used to remember your preferences or understand how visitors use the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies — required for core website functionality, such as navigating between pages.
  • Performance cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you make, such as form inputs, to make your next visit easier.

This website does not use cookies to serve third-party advertising.

3. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies; however, doing so may affect the functionality of certain parts of this website, such as the online booking form.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as font or map providers) that set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Please check this page occasionally for updates.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at +971-00-000-0000 or visit Orion Fx, Sharjah, UAE.
